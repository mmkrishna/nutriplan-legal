Subscriptions

How do I restore NutriPlan Plus on a new device?

Sign in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with, then in NutriPlan tap your profile picture, then the gear icon to open Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. You can also tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Plus screen.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → NutriPlan Plus and choose Cancel Subscription. While Plus is active you can also get there from NutriPlan’s Settings → Manage Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. If you don’t cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly subscription starts and your Apple Account is charged.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Why don’t my meals appear on my other device?

NutriPlan syncs your profile, meal plans, logs and grocery lists through your own iCloud account. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and give them a few minutes on Wi-Fi. Health readings are the exception: they always stay on the device where they were recorded.

How do I delete my data?

In NutriPlan, open Settings and tap Delete Account (or Delete My Data if you haven’t signed in). This permanently erases your account and your data on the device and in your iCloud. It can’t be undone. If you have a subscription, cancel it separately as described above.

How do I connect or disconnect Apple Health?

To connect, open NutriPlan’s Settings and tap Sync with Apple Health. NutriPlan only reads your weight and blood glucose, and never writes to Health. To change access later, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps → NutriPlan.

Meal plans

How do I stop seeing a recipe in my plan?

Tap the thumbs-down (Not for Me) on the meal, or touch and hold the meal and choose Not for Me. To bring it back, open Settings → Not for Me and tap Suggest Again.

Why are some recipes missing?

NutriPlan leaves out recipes that don’t fit the diet and allergies in your profile. To change them, tap your profile picture, then Edit Profile.
