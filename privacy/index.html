NutriPlan Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

NutriPlan is a meal-planning and nutrition-tracking app for iPhone and iPad, developed by Murali Krishna M (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

At a glance

  • Your profile, meal logs and plans stay on your device and in your own iCloud account. We cannot see them.
  • Health readings, including anything read from Apple Health, stay on your device only. They are never sent to iCloud, to us, or to anyone else.
  • We don’t show ads, don’t use analytics or tracking, and never sell your information.
  • You can delete your account and all your NutriPlan data at any time from Settings → Delete Account in the app.

Information the app handles

Profile and preferences you enter

During setup and in your profile you can enter your name, age, gender, height, weight, goal weight, activity level, goals, health conditions, allergies, diet and cuisine preferences, meals per day, cooking time, daily budget, reminder preferences and an optional profile photo. The app uses these to calculate your nutrition targets and recommend meals.

Meals, plans and grocery lists

Meals you mark as eaten (with their nutrition), meals you skip or choose, recipes you favorite or mark “Not for me”, grocery list items and the number of water glasses you log.

Health readings

Weight, blood sugar and HbA1c readings that you log in the app or allow it to read from Apple Health.

Account information

When you first use the app, it creates an anonymous account so your session can be identified. If you choose to sign in, it also handles your email address (for email sign-in) or the identifier and, if you share them, the name and email that Apple provides with Sign in with Apple. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.

Barcode scans

When you scan a food barcode, the camera image is processed on your device to read the barcode. Only the barcode number is sent to look up the product; camera images are never stored or uploaded.

Purchases

NutriPlan Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive confirmation of your subscription status, but never your payment details.

Where your information is kept

  • On your device. Everything above is stored locally in the app.
  • In your iCloud. If you’re signed in to iCloud, your profile, meal logs, plans and grocery lists are synced through Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit) to your private iCloud database, and your favorite and hidden recipes through iCloud key-value storage, so they’re available on your other devices. This data is stored in your iCloud account under Apple’s privacy policy; we have no access to it.
  • Health readings stay on this device. They are kept in a separate, device-only store and are never synced to iCloud or sent off your device.
  • Account records. Your account identifier, and your email address if you sign in with email or share it through Sign in with Apple, are stored by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • A few small values, such as today’s water count, your next planned meal and your subscription status, are shared with NutriPlan’s Home Screen widgets through storage on your device.

Apple Health

With your permission, NutriPlan reads your weight and blood glucose from Apple Health so you can track them alongside your meal plan. NutriPlan does not write any data to Apple Health.

  • Health data is used only to show your readings and trends in the app.
  • It is stored only on your device, never in iCloud, and is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • It is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and is never sold.
  • You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Services we use

NutriPlan relies on a few services to work. Each receives only what it needs and processes it under its own privacy policy. Like any internet service, they can see your device’s IP address when the app contacts them.

  • Apple: iCloud sync, App Store purchases and Sign in with Apple. Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase: account sign-in (Firebase Authentication) and delivery of the recipe catalogue and recipe images (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage). The recipe catalogue is read-only; no personal data is stored in it. Firebase privacy information.
  • Open Food Facts: product lookup for scanned barcodes. Only the barcode number is sent. Open Food Facts Privacy Policy.
  • Unsplash: some recipe photos are loaded from Unsplash’s image servers. Unsplash Privacy Policy.

These providers may process information on servers outside your country.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t include advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don’t sell, rent or share your personal information for marketing.
  • We don’t send push notifications from a server. Meal, water and shopping reminders are scheduled on your device.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan food barcodes, processed on your device.
  • Photos: only the single photo you choose for your profile. The app doesn’t browse your library.
  • Health: to read weight and blood glucose, as described above.
  • Notifications: for the reminders you turn on.

Each permission is optional; you can change it at any time in iPhone Settings.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account (or Delete My Data if you haven’t signed in). This permanently deletes your NutriPlan account from Firebase Authentication, revokes Sign in with Apple access if you used it, and erases your profile, logs, plans, favorite and hidden recipes, grocery lists and health readings from your device and your iCloud. It can’t be undone.

  • Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a NutriPlan Plus subscription. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.
  • Readings stored in Apple Health belong to the Health app and are not deleted; manage them in the Health app.
  • Deleting the app removes the data on that device; data in iCloud remains until you delete it in the app or through iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

We keep your account record only for as long as your account exists.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict its processing. Most of your information is held on your device and in your iCloud, where you can view, edit and delete it directly in the app. For anything else, or for help, contact us at the address below. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

NutriPlan is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help delete it.

Security

Information sent between the app and the services above is encrypted in transit. Data on your device and in iCloud is protected by Apple’s platform security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit what leaves your device to what the app needs to work.

Not medical advice

NutriPlan provides general nutrition guidance and meal suggestions. It does not diagnose, treat or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
